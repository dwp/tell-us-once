If you think an address or council is wrong

If the person who died lived at another address where they received local council services, you can add that address and choose to notify that council.

For example, they might have lived in a care home but reveived council services at their home address.

If the person who died had a single address where they received local council services, and the address here is wrong, you need to contact the registrar to record their correct address.
